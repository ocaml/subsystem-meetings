The type-system meeting discusses PRs and issues touching the github/ocaml type-checker.

This is typically a place where people who have worked on the
type-system on their own (to diagnose/fix a bug, submit a PR, review
a PR, etc.) discuss remaining questions, get opinions from others. We
can also use it to converge quicker to decisions when online
discussions themselves are inconclusive.

The meeting places an emphasis on changes that make the type-system
easier to understand and maintain (for example refactoring changes to
clarify and enforce level invariants), and building more widely shared
expertise of the type-system codebase.

Meeting frequency: every month.
