- tuple projections ? (new information!)
  https://github.com/ocaml/RFCs/pull/57#issuecomment-3587146365
  
Gabriel: flexible tuples do not give the most general type when you have tuples that mix labeled and unlabeled components.

Jacques: this was already clear to me!

Gabriel: so for now the consensus (we talked about this with Alistair) is to restrict the focus to labelled projections.

Alistair: it was new to me that introduced labelled tuples means that there is not a principal type for numeric projections.

Gabriel: I also thought about restricting the language to forbid mixing numeric and labeled components.

Alistair: I've used the approach of labeling only components with un-informative types, and keeping no labels on elements with an informative type.

Jacques: or there is the option of requiring that the unlabeled fields come first.

Richard: one way of thinking about unlabeled components of a tuple is that they have a distinguished label that is stored in zero characters. But under that view, we can repeat labels. In fact the implementation with JS supports repeating labels.

Jacques: having projections by indices is a feature that people are not asking for, but having them would be very cool.

Samuel: one case I found where it was very useful was with Menhir, which has no convenient syntax to unpack symbol names.

- removal of `let rec (module M) = ...`

Jacques: for historic reasons I'm quite fond of recursive modules in general. Actually, full-fledged syntactic modules are pretty heavy syntactically, and somehow `let rec (module M)` has the advantage of being a bit lighter in that respect.
